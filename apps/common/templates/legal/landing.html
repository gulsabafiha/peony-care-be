Udufood

Surplus meals, shared with neighbours.

Udufood is a Singapore app that connects restaurants with leftover food to people nearby who can use a complementary meal.

Get the app Contact us

Receivers

See meals near you, claim one a day, and pick it up with a QR code. Sign in with your mobile number.

Restaurants

List surplus food before closing time. Receivers collect it from your shop — less waste, more meals shared.

Donors

Sponsor meals at partner restaurants so more people can eat, even when there is no leftover stock.

How it works

  1. Download Udufood on Google Play and register with a Singapore mobile number.
  2. Restaurants post leftover dishes. Receivers browse what is nearby.
  3. Claim a meal, show your QR at pickup, and collect it in the stated window.
